━━ PACING PRINCIPLES ━━
- Cut on action, not before or after it.
- J-cuts: let the audio from the next scene arrive before the picture cuts — creates pull.
- L-cuts: let the current audio extend over the next picture — creates continuity.
- Use holds deliberately: a static frame after high-energy content gives the viewer a breath and focuses attention.
