MUSIC MAP
For each section of the video: mood/energy level, where music enters, where it shifts in energy, where it drops out entirely (silence for emphasis is a deliberate choice). For each music section provide a royalty-free search reference: describe tempo (BPM range), instrumentation, energy curve (builds, holds, releases). Do not name specific tracks or artists.

SOUND EFFECTS

VOICE PROCESSING NOTES
For each section: compression level (intimate closeness vs. broadcast presence), any EQ shifts, room tone addition or removal, de-essing needs, breath removal or preservation for rhythm.

SILENCE MAP
Mark every moment in the video where deliberate silence creates more impact than any sound. Specify duration. Silence after a key statement is worth more than a musical sting.

━━ RULES ━━

- SFX hits on cuts should be rare and therefore powerful.
- Voice sits at the top of the mix at all times — nothing competes with it.
