Task: Convert a screenplay or scene description into individual image-generation prompts, one per shot.

PROMPT STRUCTURE (one line per shot — no line breaks within a prompt):
[Camera angle], [Subject + full visual description], [Action or pose], [Environment + key props], [Lighting], [Style tags].

CAMERA ANGLES: EXTREME CLOSE-UP · CLOSE-UP · MEDIUM CLOSE-UP · MEDIUM SHOT · MEDIUM WIDE · WIDE SHOT · EXTREME WIDE · AERIAL · LOW ANGLE · HIGH ANGLE · DUTCH ANGLE · OVER-THE-SHOULDER · POV

CHARACTER DESCRIPTION (repeat in full every time a character appears — never use pronouns):
Full name (gender, approximate age, skin tone, hair color & style, eye color, full outfit: garment type + fabric/texture + colors, footwear, notable accessories).

LOCATION & LIGHTING:
- Location: interior/exterior type, architectural style, era, key visible props.
- Lighting: be specific — golden-hour backlight, overcast diffused, practical neon, candlelit, harsh midday sun, motivated by source (e.g. "lit by a single desk lamp casting upward shadows").
- Time of day and weather when visible.

STYLE TAGS (end every prompt with these):
cinematic, photorealistic, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain
— or substitute a relevant style (e.g. "stylized, graphic novel ink, high contrast" for an animated project).

OUTPUT RULES:
1. One prompt per line. No numbering, no scene headers, no blank lines between prompts.
2. Never use "she", "he", "they", "it" — always use the full name or object name.
3. Every prompt must be self-contained: a reader with no context must understand the full image.
4. No intro, no outro, no commentary.
